FAQ

Before you call

Still unsure? Ring 0800 WANT GAS and ask the team.

0800 926 8427
What areas do you cover?

We are based at 140 Lower Dent Street in Whangārei and work right across Northland. Water delivery runs in set zones around Whangārei and Dargaville; see the delivery maps.

How do I get a quote?

Call 0800 WANT GAS (0800 926 8427) or email info@northlandpg.co.nz with the address and what you need done. For new builds and renovations, get in touch at the planning stage; we can usually save you money with good planning early.

Can one company really do the whole job?

Yes. We do the earthworks, drainlaying, wastewater, plumbing, gas and hot water, so a new build or rural section can go from first trench to final fit-off with one team and one point of contact.

Do you prepare wastewater reports for building consent?

Yes. Wastewater reports are often required with a building consent application for rural sites. Talk to us before you submit plans to council.

Are your people qualified?

Our plumbers, gasfitters and drainlayers are qualified and registered, our drainlayers are certified by the Plumbers, Gasfitters and Drainlayers Board, and gas work is certified and signed off. We are Master Plumbers members and hold Sitewise Gold for 2025/26.

What is included in the hot water deals?

The Rinnai A26 conversion ($3,200 incl GST) replaces your old cylinder with instant gas hot water. The mains pressure upgrade ($2,950 incl GST) supplies and installs an electric mains pressure cylinder with valves, seismic restraints, tempering valve and removal of the old cylinder, connected to existing plumbing in the same location. Electrical work is not included and terms apply.

Is your delivered water safe to drink?

Yes. We are a Taumata Arowai registered water carrier and deliver clean, potable water, up to 10,000 litres per load.

What are your hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 4.30pm. Call 0800 WANT GAS to book a job, or 0800 WANT H2O for water delivery.
